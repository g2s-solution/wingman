# Code of conduct

Wingman is a small open-source project. We want it to be a place where people can ask questions, report problems and send changes without being treated badly.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to Claude Code.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person. Assume good intent and explain your reasoning.
- Keep discussion on topic and in the open, in issues and pull requests.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Threats, intimidation or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as an address, email or account details, without their consent.
- Posting secrets, tokens or logs that contain them, yours or anyone else's.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or spam.

## Scope

This applies to every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these rules. Serious or repeated violations lead to a ban from the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to an owner of the `g2s-solution` GitHub organization, or use the private vulnerability reporting route described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) if it also concerns security. Do not post personal details in public issues, and do not ask others for theirs: a report should include only what is needed (links to the comments involved). Reports are handled in confidence.
